YOUR ENTRIES

WHAT IT IS

A private dashboard to track and monitor a personal set of holdings — the coins BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL, PAXG, XAUT and the stock PANW. You record the purchases you made (or are considering), and it looks up the price on that exact date, then displays what each holding is worth now, your profit or loss, and how it all breaks down — in AED, CAD and USDT at once.

It runs entirely in your browser. There is no account and no server: your data stays on this device and only moves in a backup file you choose to carry.

WHAT IT ISN'T

  • Not a wallet or an exchange. It holds no coins and moves no money — it only records what you tell it and shows the maths.
  • Not a live trading terminal. Prices are historical closing values for tracking, not a real-time order book.
  • Not financial advice. The value and profit/loss figures are estimates from past prices, not brokerage statements or recommendations.
  • Not a synced cloud account. Nothing is stored online; there is no login that follows you between devices — you move your own encrypted backup.
  • Not a full market tracker. It follows only the seven assets above, by design.

ADDING A PURCHASE

  1. Pick the display currency in the top-right (AED / CAD / USDT). New amounts are typed in this currency.
  2. Open the Entries tab and choose the coin or stock, the amount you spent, and the date you bought.
  3. Optionally set a time — crypto then uses that hour's price; stocks use the day's close.
  4. Press + Add entry. The app fetches that moment's price and starts tracking the holding from there.
  5. Reorder entries with the ▲ / ▼ controls, or remove one with ×.

READING THE DASHBOARD

  • Invested / Value now / Profit-loss — your totals in the selected currency, with profit or loss against the money you put in.
  • What you own, in each currency — the same totals shown in AED, CAD and USDT at once.
  • Assets — include or exclude — untick any asset to drop it from everything: totals, allocation, the breakdown and both graphs.
  • Chart range — 1D through 5Y changes the window on the price and portfolio charts.
  • Tap or hover the charts to read exact values at any point.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

  • Encrypted on your device. Everything you save is encrypted with your passkey using AES-256-GCM, with the key stretched from your passkey by PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations) and a random salt — all in your browser.
  • The passkey is never stored or sent. Unlocking simply tries to decrypt; a wrong passkey fails and reveals nothing. This is also why there is no recovery — forget it and the data is gone.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. The only network calls are read-only price and exchange-rate lookups, which carry none of your data.
  • The code is public, your data isn't. Even someone reading the source or the stored file sees only ciphertext — useless without your passkey.
  • Moving it is on your terms. Backup (top-right) downloads the encrypted file; Restore from a backup file on the lock screen loads it on another device, where the same passkey unlocks it. Lock wipes the key from memory until you re-enter it.
  • Shared browser? Anyone using this browser profile can reach the lock screen, but not your data without the passkey. Clearing the browser's site data erases the vault here — keep a backup.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Crypto prices come from Binance; PANW from stockanalysis.com (split-adjusted); exchange rates from the ECB via Frankfurter. AED is pegged 3.6725, USDT counts 1:1 with USD.
  • XAUT history on the exchange starts March 2026, so its line and any earlier buy date are limited to that window. PAXG (also gold) reaches back to 2020.
  • These are estimates from historical closing prices — not brokerage records or advice.
